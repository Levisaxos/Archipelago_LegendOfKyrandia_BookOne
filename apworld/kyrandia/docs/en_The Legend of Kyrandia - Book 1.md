# The Legend of Kyrandia - Book 1 (Archipelago) — DRAFT

## What is this?
An Archipelago randomizer world for the 1992 point-and-click adventure
*The Legend of Kyrandia: Book One*. Every pickup, NPC turn-in and puzzle reward
becomes an Archipelago **location**; the items they normally give are shuffled
into the multiworld.

## How does it work?
You explore Kyrandia roughly in the vanilla order, but *what you receive* at each
spot is decided by Archipelago. Progress is gated by the real game items the
original used at each transition — the **Saw** repairs the bridge, the **Flute**
gets you past the grotto, the **Amulet** powers the spells that cross the chasm,
and the **Crown + Sceptre + Royal Chalice** complete the Kyragem ending (the goal).

## Status
**Pre-alpha.** The logic is a first-pass draft pending verification against a real
playthrough, and the forked game client that connects to a server does not exist
yet. The world generates valid seeds for testing and tracking today.

## Goal
Place the Crown, Sceptre and Royal Chalice in the Kyragem chamber.
